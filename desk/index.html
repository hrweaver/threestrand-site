Threestrand for Mac

Answer requests from the people you're paired with right from your menu bar, without picking up your phone. You can also block websites on your Mac if you want to.

Download for Mac

Version 0.2.15 · macOS 13 or later · Early version

Set it up

  1. Open the download and drag Threestrand into your Applications folder, then open it from there. If you open it somewhere else first, it offers to move itself.
  2. Open Threestrand. Its icon shows up at the top of your screen, in the menu bar. Click it to see a code.
  3. Scan the code with your phone. On iPhone, use the camera. On Android, open Threestrand, then Settings, Partners, Connect a computer. Check that the four numbers match, and tap Connect.

No account needed. Your Mac joins whatever your phone already uses.

Blocking websites (optional)

Click Threestrand in the menu bar, then Set up next to website blocking. Pick sites and when they're blocked. macOS asks you to allow it twice, once in System Settings and once to filter network content. Your list stays on your Mac. The person you're paired with only sees a site when you ask for it.
