Phones away at dinner, everyone at the table.

A parent presses Family time and the whole family's phones are away until dinner's over. Apps close, notifications wait, calls still ring. Yours too, not just the kids'.

Android and iPhone. The family can be on either.

How it works

One button. Everyone's phone, including yours.

This isn't a parental control. The parent who starts family time loses their apps too.

Set up your household

One code gets everyone in: both parents, the teens, anyone else in the house. Teens 13 and up.

Press Family time

A parent picks how long, and everyone's phones go quiet together. Apps close and notifications wait. Calls ring the whole time.

No locks, just witnesses

A parent can end it early, and everyone's phone shows who did and how early. Nobody gets stuck. Nobody gets away with it quietly either.
