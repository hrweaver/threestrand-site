How it works

Decide once, with someone on your side.

Every app blocker has the same problem: the person who sets it up can turn it off. Threestrand puts that switch in someone else's hands, so the time you meant for your family actually goes to them.

Pick a partner

Send a link to someone you trust. One tap and you're paired. A spouse, a roommate, a friend, or a few of them.

Pick what stays open

The map, the bank, the camera, the apps you actually need. Everything else is blocked, including apps you install later.

Ask when you need it

Open something blocked and send a quick request. They say yes, or not this time, and it opens for as long as they said.

What a partner sees: that your blocking is on, and the requests you send. Not which apps you use, not for how long, not what you browse. And deleting Threestrand always works. It stays an emergency exit, and your partners are simply told.

The whole house

Yours too, not just the kids'.

Want everyone in on it? A parent can press Family time and the whole household's phones go quiet together, their own included. Apps close, notifications wait, calls still ring.

Less phone. More people.

Try it for 7 days. It takes a few minutes to set up, and it stays out of your way after that.
