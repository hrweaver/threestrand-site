Already have Threestrand? Open it
  1. 1

    Get Threestrand

    Android or iPhone.

  2. 2

    Open it and pick “Tapped a card at a table?”

    Your name, then two permissions: Screen Time on iPhone, or app blocking and notification access on Android. That's how the apps close. It doesn't read your messages or photos. About two minutes, once.

  3. 3

    Hold the top of your phone on the card

    Pick how long. Your apps stay closed until the time's up, or until you leave. Calls still ring. Texts wait on Android; on iPhone you choose whether Messages closes too.

  4. 4

    Everyone at the table can join

    Each person holds their phone on the card. Their apps close too, for the same amount of time.
