Phones down, coffee up.

You're at a table with a Threestrand card. This is the whole thing. Already have the app? Skip to step 3.

  1. 1

    Get Threestrand

    Android or iPhone.

    Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play
  2. 2

    Open it and pick “I'm at a table with a tag”

    About two minutes. It asks for a couple of permissions so it can actually hold your apps closed.

  3. 3

    Hold the top of your phone on the card

    Pick how long. Your apps stay closed for the length of a coffee. Calls still work.

  4. 4

    Across from someone?

    Their phone goes on the card too, and the table goes quiet.

That's it. Enjoy your coffee. Want it for more than coffee? That's the rest of Threestrand.
