Why the name

A cord of three strands is not quickly broken.

A strand on its own wears thin. Woven in with others, it holds. That's the whole idea behind Threestrand. Not a stronger lock. More people in it with you.

Someone to answer to

When you want in to something you blocked, you ask someone you chose. They say yes, or not this time. That small moment of asking is what changes things.

Phones away, together

Put phones away with whoever you're with, for as long as you all said. A dinner, a study night, a living room, a table.

On the phone you already have

Android or iPhone, old or new. No new phone needed.

Human connection, without all the noise.

Together

Tap to join.

Threestrand has a physical side: a small tag, on the back of a phone or on a table. Hold your phone to it and you're in. A partner's tag says yes to a request on the spot. A table's tag puts phones away with whoever's there.

A few tables in the Texas Hill Country and around San Antonio have one. Want one for your shop or your group? Request a call.
Every day

People, not willpower.

A limit you can lift by yourself isn't much of a limit. Threestrand puts people you chose between you and the apps that waste your time.

Pick a partner

Send a link to someone you trust. One tap and you're paired. A spouse, a roommate, a friend, or a few of them.

Choose what to block

The apps that take your time without giving much back. They stay blocked everywhere on the phone, not just on the home screen.

Ask when you need it

Open something blocked and send a quick request. They say yes, or not this time, and it opens for as long as they said.

What a partner sees: that your blocking is on, and the requests you send. Not which apps you use, not for how long, not what you browse. And deleting Threestrand always works. It stays an emergency exit, and your partners are simply told.

What we're after

A little more of each other.

Phones are useful. The map, the bank, the camera, the calls. The time worth protecting is the time that serves the phone instead of you.

We don't think an algorithm can hold you to a promise. People can. So Threestrand is small on purpose: no ads, no streaks, no scores, nothing that shames you for a bad day. The people you choose are on your side of the table, not across it. It works on whatever phone you already have.

The best thing it can do is get out of your way. We measure success by how little you need us.

Pick a partner. Put the phone down.

It takes a few minutes to set up, and it stays out of your way after that.
